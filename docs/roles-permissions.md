## Role Overview

IMS has two roles. Your role is selected during account registration and cannot be changed after signup.

| Role | Primary purpose |
|------|----------------|
| **Intern** | Logs attendance, tracks OJT progress, submits requests, manages personal documents |
| **Supervisor** | Oversees assigned interns, approves requests, assigns tasks, manages schedules |

## Department Access

Both interns and supervisors belong to one of three departments. Access is scoped to users within the same department context:

| Department | Code |
|------------|------|
| International NOC | ISOC |
| Regional Service Center | RSC |
| Customer Network & Facilities Management | CNFM |

A supervisor only sees interns assigned to them directly — not all interns in their department.
