## Creating an Account

Registration uses a guided multi-step flow. The steps you see depend on your role.

### Step 1 — Account Details

Fill in the following fields:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Name** | Your full name |
| **Email** | A valid email address you can access for OTP verification |
| **Password** | Minimum 8 characters |
| **Confirm Password** | Must match the password above |
| **Role** | **Intern** or **Supervisor** |
| **Department** | Supervisors only — select ISOC, RSC, or CNFM |

Click **Continue**.

- If you selected **Supervisor**, you skip to email verification immediately.
- If you selected **Intern**, you proceed to the OJT Setup step.

---

### Step 2 — OJT Setup (Intern only)

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Total OJT Hours** | Required hours set by your school (e.g., 500) |
| **Start Date** | Your official OJT start date |
| **Department** | The department where you are assigned |
| **Course** | Your degree program — type to search from the catalog |
| **School** | Your university — type to search from the catalog |

Click **Complete Signup** when all fields are filled.

---

### Step 3 — Email Verification

A **6-digit OTP** is sent to your registered email address.

1. Enter the code in the **OTP Code** field.
2. Click **Verify OTP**.
3. If the code expires before you enter it, click **Resend OTP** to get a new one.

> Verify promptly — OTP codes are time-limited.

---

### Step 4 — Account Ready

Once verified, a success screen confirms your account is active. Click **Go to Login** to sign in.

---

## Logging In

1. Open the IMS login page.
2. Enter your registered **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Log In**.
4. You are redirected to your role-specific dashboard automatically.
   - Interns land on the **Intern Dashboard**.
   - Supervisors land on the **Supervisor Dashboard**.

---

## Forgot Password

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password?**
2. Make sure your email address is entered in the Email field first.
3. Click the link — if an account exists for that email, recovery instructions are sent.

> If the email field is empty when you click Forgot Password, you will be prompted to enter it first.
