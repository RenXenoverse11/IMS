## Account & Login

**Q: I entered the correct password but cannot log in.**
A: Double-check that your email address is typed correctly. Passwords are case-sensitive. If you still cannot log in, use **Forgot Password?** on the login page to reset your credentials.

**Q: My OTP expired before I could verify my email.**
A: On the Verify Email step, click **Resend OTP**. A new 6-digit code will be sent to your registered email address.

**Q: Can I change my role after registering?**
A: No. Role selection happens at registration and cannot be changed. Create a new account with the correct role if needed.

**Q: I don't see my supervisor in the Supervisors tab under Settings.**
A: Your supervisor must assign you to their supervision from the **Intern Management** page. Contact your supervisor and ask them to complete the assignment.

## OJT Hours & Dashboard

**Q: My hours look lower than expected on the dashboard.**
A: Check whether you have an active session still running. Hours from an active session are counted live but are only saved permanently when you clock out. Also confirm your Total OJT Hours and Start Date are correctly set — these are entered during signup and affect all calculations.

**Q: What is the difference between Rendered and Projected progress?**
A: **Rendered** counts only your submitted time log hours plus any currently active session. **Projected** adds future approved and pending overtime/absence requests on top of rendered hours. Use Projected to see your estimated progress once pending requests are resolved.

**Q: My estimated end date seems too far in the future.**
A: The estimate is based on your remaining hours divided by an 8-hour working day, calculated forward from today using your assigned schedule (skipping days off). If your actual daily hours differ or you have many absence requests pending, the estimate will adjust once those requests are approved or rejected.

**Q: My dashboard shows I am at 100% but I still see hours remaining.**
A: If your rendered hours meet or exceed your required total, the progress bar shows 100% and remaining is set to 0. If hours remaining still shows a value, check that your Total OJT Hours setting is correct.

## Time Log

**Q: I forgot to clock out yesterday. What should I do?**
A: Submit an **Override Request** from the Time Log page. Enter the correct date, the time you actually arrived and left, and a brief reason. Your supervisor will review and approve the correction.

**Q: Can I edit a time log entry directly?**
A: Existing entries cannot be edited directly. Submit an Override Request to correct any entry. The supervisor's approval replaces the incorrect record.

**Q: The lunch break toggle — what exactly does it deduct?**
A: It deducts a standard lunch period from the total hours calculated for that session. Enable it if your internship schedule includes an unpaid lunch break that should not count toward your OJT hours.

**Q: How do I export my attendance records?**
A: Click **Export Attendance** in the Time Log page, select the month, and a PDF is generated and downloaded automatically.

## Requests

**Q: I submitted an absence request but my projected hours did not change.**
A: Absence requests reduce projected hours only when they are in **Approved** or **Pending** status and the request date is today or in the future. Past-dated approved absences are not added to the projection since those hours have already affected your logged total.

**Q: My overtime request was approved but my hours did not increase.**
A: The same logic applies — only future-dated approved/pending overtime affects the Projected view. Switch the dashboard toggle to **Projected** to see the effect. Rendered hours only update when you actually log the overtime time.

**Q: Can I delete a request that is still Pending?**
A: You can archive a pending request, which removes it from the active list. To delete it permanently, archive it first and then use the delete action on the archived item.

**Q: What happens to an Expired request?**
A: Expired requests had their date pass before a supervisor decision was made. They no longer affect any hour calculations and can be archived or deleted.

## Documents

**Q: What file types can I upload?**
A: Most common file formats are supported (PDF, images, Word documents, spreadsheets, etc.). Profile photos specifically require JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF at a maximum of 5 MB.

**Q: Can I recover a deleted document?**
A: No. Deletions are permanent. Download or back up important files before removing them.

**Q: I shared a document but the recipient says they cannot see it.**
A: Open the Share dialog for that document and confirm the visibility mode includes the recipient. If you used **Specific users**, verify the correct user was selected. If you used **Everyone except**, check they were not accidentally added to the exclusion list.

## Supervisor-Specific

**Q: An intern does not appear on my dashboard.**
A: The intern must be assigned to your supervision from the **Intern Management** page. Go to Intern Management, find the intern, and assign them to yourself.

**Q: Can I approve my own requests?**
A: No. Supervisors do not submit absence or overtime requests — that feature is for interns only. If a supervisor needs time off, that is handled outside IMS.

**Q: I changed an intern's schedule but their estimated end date did not update.**
A: The estimated end date recalculates the next time the intern's dashboard loads. Ask the intern to refresh their dashboard.
