1. Overview

The Internship Management System (IMS) is a web-based platform for managing On-the-Job Training (OJT) programs in a telecom-grade internship environment.

IMS brings together everything needed to run a structured internship program — from daily attendance and progress tracking to formal request approvals and document management — in one secure, role-based platform.

Intern

  • Log daily attendance (clock in/out)
  • Track OJT hours and progress
  • Submit absence and overtime requests
  • Upload and manage documents
  • View assigned tasks

Supervisor

  • Monitor all assigned interns
  • Approve or reject requests
  • Assign tasks and manage projects
  • Review and correct time logs
  • Configure intern schedules

2. Getting Started

Creating an Account

Registration uses a guided multi-step flow. Steps vary by role.

Step 1 — Account Details

FieldNotes
NameYour full name
EmailA valid email you can access — required for OTP verification
PasswordMinimum 8 characters
Confirm PasswordMust match the password above
RoleSelect Intern or Supervisor
DepartmentSupervisors only — select ISOC, RSC, or CNFM

Click Continue. Supervisors skip to email verification. Interns proceed to OJT Setup.

Step 2 — OJT Setup (Intern only)

FieldNotes
Total OJT HoursRequired hours from your school (e.g., 500)
Start DateYour official OJT start date
DepartmentThe department you are assigned to
CourseYour degree program — type to search
SchoolYour university — type to search

Click Complete Signup.

Step 3 — Email Verification

  1. A 6-digit OTP is sent to your registered email address.
  2. Enter the code in the OTP Code field.
  3. Click Verify OTP.
  4. If the code expires, click Resend OTP to get a new one.
OTP codes are time-limited — verify promptly after receiving the email.

Step 4 — Account Ready

A success screen confirms your account is active. Click Go to Login.

Logging In

  1. Open the IMS login page.
  2. Enter your registered Email and Password.
  3. Click Log In.
  4. You are redirected to your role-specific dashboard automatically.

Forgot Password

  1. On the login page, enter your email address in the Email field.
  2. Click Forgot Password?
  3. If an account exists for that email, recovery instructions are sent.

3. Intern Guide

Dashboard

Your home page — a live overview of your OJT progress and recent activity.

Welcome Banner

Displays your name, Intern role badge, department, course, and school. The Rendered / Projected toggle in the top-right controls which hours count toward your progress:

ModeWhat it counts
RenderedSubmitted time logs plus any session currently active
ProjectedRendered hours plus future approved and pending overtime/absence requests

Stat Cards

CardDescription
Hours NeededYour total required OJT hours
Hours CompletedHours logged so far; updates live during an active session
Hours RemainingHours left until your OJT is finished
Working Days NeededEstimated working days remaining based on an 8-hour day

Progress Bar

Shows your completion percentage. The footer displays hours remaining versus total required. Once you reach 100%, it displays Completed.

Estimated End Date

A projected OJT completion date calculated from your start date, remaining hours, and schedule. After completion this changes to Completed On showing your actual finish date.

Recent Activity

A feed of your latest log-in, log-out, and request decision events. Click View on a request event to jump to that request on the Requests page.

Assigned Tasks

Lists up to 10 tasks assigned by your supervisor, sorted by due date. Click View to open the task in the Activity Log.

Time Log

Record your daily attendance by clocking in and out.

Clocking In

  1. Navigate to Time Log from the sidebar.
  2. Select the Date (defaults to today).
  3. Set your Time In.
  4. Toggle Lunch Break if you want a lunch period deducted.
  5. Click Log In — your session starts.
If you leave and return to IMS during an active session, elapsed time continues accumulating and is shown live on your Dashboard.

Clocking Out

  1. With an active session running, set your Time Out.
  2. Click Log Out — the entry is saved and hours are added to your total.

Time Log History

ColumnDescription
DateDay of the entry
Time InClock-in time
Time OutClock-out time
Total HoursHours worked that day
StatusRecorded for all saved entries

Use the filter tabs to switch between Entries (regular logs) and Override Requests (corrections).

Override Request

Submit this when you missed a clock-in or clock-out, or need to correct an existing entry.

  1. Click Request Override in the Time Log page.
  2. Enter the correct Date, Time In, Time Out, and a Reason.
  3. Submit — your supervisor will approve or reject the correction.

Exporting Attendance

Click Export Attendance, select a month, and a PDF of your time log is downloaded automatically.

Activity Log

View tasks and work logs assigned to you by your supervisor.

  • Expand any entry to see full details, attachments, and notes.
  • Tasks show their status, priority, and due date.
  • Clicking a task from Dashboard → Assigned Tasks opens the Activity Log with that task highlighted.

Requests

Formally notify your supervisor of absences or extra hours worked.

Request Types

TypeWhen to use
AbsenceA planned or past day you could not attend your internship
OvertimeExtra hours worked beyond your regular schedule that should count toward your OJT total

Submitting a Request

  1. Navigate to Requests.
  2. Click New Request.
  3. Select the Request Type.
  4. Set the Date and fill in required details.
  5. Click Submit.

Request Statuses

StatusMeaning
PendingSubmitted — awaiting supervisor review
ApprovedAccepted — hours are factored into your OJT progress
RejectedDeclined — does not affect your hours
ExpiredRequest date passed without a decision
ArchivedManually moved to archive by you

Managing Requests

  • Archive — move a resolved request out of the active list.
  • Bulk archive — select multiple requests and archive them at once.
  • Delete — permanently removes an archived request. This cannot be undone.

Documents

  2. Click Upload and select a file — or Add Link to attach a URL.
  3. Choose a destination folder (defaults to My Documents root).
  4. Confirm the upload.

Settings

Update your Full Name, Email, Phone, and Department. Upload a Profile Photo (JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF; max 5 MB). Click Save Changes.

Enter your current password and a new password (minimum 8 characters) to update your login credentials.

Intern Management

Assigning Interns

Go to Intern Management and assign interns to your supervision. An intern not assigned to any supervisor will not appear on any supervisor's dashboard.

Each intern can have a custom schedule that affects time log defaults, working day calculations, and absence/overtime hour projections.

FieldDescription

Projects

  1. Navigate to Projects.
  2. Click New Project.
  3. Enter a project name, description, and optional deadline.
  4. Open the project and use the assign controls to add interns.

Each project shows associated tasks, their statuses, and which interns are working on them.

Time Log Review

Viewing an Intern's Time Log

Select an intern from the list in the supervisor Time Log page to see their full attendance history: all recorded log-in/log-out entries, daily totals, and override requests.

Reviewing Override Requests

  1. Open an intern's Time Log.
  2. Switch to the Override Requests tab.
  3. Review the submitted date, times, and reason.
  4. Click Approve or Reject.
DecisionEffect
ApprovedCorrected entry is added to the intern's time log and hour total
RejectedOriginal record stands; intern is notified

Activity Log Management

Creating a Task

  1. Navigate to Activity Log.
  2. Click New Task.
  3. Fill in title, description, assigned intern(s), due date, and priority.
  4. Save — the task appears on the intern's Dashboard and Activity Log.

Task Statuses

StatusMeaning
PendingNot yet started

Navigate to Requests to review all pending intern requests. Click Approve or Reject on each card.

Approve AbsenceDeducts equivalent hours from the intern's projected total
RejectNo change to hours; intern is notified via their activity feed

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Account & Login

I entered the correct password but cannot log in.

Double-check that your email address is typed correctly — passwords are case-sensitive. If you still cannot log in, use Forgot Password? on the login page.

My OTP expired before I could verify my email.

On the Verify Email step, click Resend OTP. A new 6-digit code will be sent to your registered email address.

Can I change my role after registering?

No. Role selection is permanent at registration. Create a new account with the correct role if needed.

I don't see my supervisor in Settings → Supervisors.

Your supervisor must assign you from their Intern Management page. Contact your supervisor and ask them to complete the assignment.

OJT Hours & Dashboard

My hours look lower than expected on the dashboard.

Check whether you have an active session still running. Hours from an active session count live but are only saved permanently after you clock out. Also confirm your Total OJT Hours and Start Date are set correctly from your signup.

What is the difference between Rendered and Projected progress?

Rendered counts only submitted time log hours plus any currently active session. Projected adds pending and approved future overtime/absence requests on top. Switch to Projected to see your estimated progress once pending requests are resolved.

My estimated end date seems too far in the future.

The estimate is based on remaining hours divided by an 8-hour working day, skipping your assigned days off. If your daily hours differ or you have many absence requests pending, the estimate adjusts once those are resolved.

Time Log

I forgot to clock out yesterday. What do I do?

Submit an Override Request from the Time Log page. Enter the correct date, actual time in and out, and a brief reason. Your supervisor will review and approve the correction.

Can I edit a time log entry directly?

Existing entries cannot be edited directly. Submit an Override Request to correct any entry.

What does the lunch break toggle deduct?

It deducts a standard lunch period from the total hours for that session. Enable it if your internship schedule includes an unpaid lunch break that should not count toward OJT hours.

Requests

I submitted an overtime request but my projected hours did not change.

Switch the Dashboard toggle to Projected — only the Projected view reflects future approved/pending requests. The Rendered view updates only when you actually log the hours.

What happens to an Expired request?

Expired requests had their date pass before a supervisor decision was made. They no longer affect any hour calculations and can be archived or deleted.

Can I delete a Pending request?

Archive it first, then use the delete action on the archived item. Deletion is permanent.

Documents

Can I recover a deleted document?

No — deletions are permanent. Download or back up important files before removing them.

I shared a document but the recipient cannot see it.

Open the Share dialog and confirm the visibility mode includes the recipient. If you used Specific users, verify the correct user was selected. If you used Everyone except, check they were not accidentally added to the exclusion list.

Supervisor-Specific

An intern does not appear on my dashboard.

Go to Intern Management and assign that intern to your supervision. Only assigned interns appear on your dashboard.

I changed an intern's schedule but their estimated end date did not update.

The estimate recalculates the next time the intern's dashboard loads. Ask the intern to refresh their page.
