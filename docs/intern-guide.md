## Dashboard

The dashboard is your home page — it shows a live overview of your OJT progress.

### Welcome Banner

Displays your name, **Intern** role badge, department, course, and school.

**Rendered / Projected toggle** — controls which hours are counted toward progress:

| Mode | What it counts |
|------|---------------|
| **Rendered** | Submitted time logs plus any session currently active |
| **Projected** | Rendered hours plus future approved and pending overtime/absence requests |

Switch modes using the toggle in the top-right corner of the banner. The Projected view is useful for estimating your completion date when you have pending requests that have not yet been decided.

---

### Stat Cards

| Card | What it shows |
|------|--------------|
| **Hours Needed** | Your total required OJT hours (set during signup) |
| **Hours Completed** | Hours logged so far; updates live while a session is active |
| **Hours Remaining** | Hours left until you finish your OJT |
| **Working Days Needed** | Estimated working days remaining based on an 8-hour day |

---

### Progress Bar

Shows your completion percentage. The footer displays hours remaining versus total required.

---

### Estimated End Date

A projected OJT completion date calculated from your start date, remaining hours, and schedule. Once you complete your OJT, this changes to **Completed On** showing your actual finish date.

---

### Recent Activity

A scrollable feed of your latest events:

- **Log In / Log Out** entries from your time log
- **Request approvals and rejections** from your supervisor

Click **View** on a request event to jump directly to that request on the Requests page.

---

### Assigned Tasks

Lists up to 10 tasks assigned to you by your supervisor, sorted by due date. Click **View** on any task to open it in the Activity Log with that task highlighted.

## Time Log

The Time Log is where you record your daily attendance.

### Clocking In

1. Navigate to **Time Log** from the sidebar.
2. Select the **Date** for your entry (defaults to today).
3. Set your **Time In**.
4. Toggle the **lunch break** option if you want a lunch break deducted from your total hours.
5. Click **Log In**. Your active session begins.

> If you leave and come back to IMS during an active session, the elapsed time continues accumulating and is reflected on your Dashboard in real time.

---

### Clocking Out

1. With an active session, set your **Time Out**.
2. Click **Log Out**. The entry is saved and hours are added to your completed total.

---

### Time Log History

Below the clock-in controls, the history table lists all your past entries:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Date | Day of the entry |
| Time In | Clock-in time |
| Time Out | Clock-out time |
| Total Hours | Hours worked that day |
| Status | Always **Recorded** for approved entries |

Use the filter tabs to switch between **Entries** (regular logs) and **Override Requests** (corrections).

---

### Override Request

Use this when you missed logging in or out, or need to correct an existing entry.

1. Click **Request Override** in the Time Log area.
2. Fill in:
   - **Date** — the day that needs correcting
   - **Time In** and **Time Out** — the correct times
   - **Reason** — a brief explanation for the correction
3. Submit. Your supervisor will review and approve or reject the request.

---

### Exporting Attendance

Click **Export Attendance** and select a month to download a PDF copy of your time log for that period.

## Activity Log

The Activity Log displays tasks and work logs associated with your internship.

- **Work logs** submitted directly or assigned by your supervisor appear here.
- Click any entry to expand it and see full details, attachments, and notes.
- Tasks assigned from the supervisor workflow show their status and due date.
- Opening a task from the **Dashboard → Assigned Tasks** panel navigates directly to that task in the Activity Log.

## Requests

Use the Requests page to formally notify your supervisor of absences or extra hours worked.

### Request Types

| Type | When to use |
|------|-------------|
| **Absence** | A planned or past day you could not attend your internship |
| **Overtime** | Extra hours worked beyond your regular schedule that should count toward your OJT total |

---

### Submitting a Request

1. Navigate to **Requests**.
2. Click **New Request**.
3. Select the **Request Type**.
4. Set the **Date** and fill in any required details.
5. Click **Submit**.

The request appears in your list with **Pending** status and is sent to your supervisor for review.

---

### Request Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Submitted — awaiting supervisor review |
| **Approved** | Accepted — hours are factored into your OJT progress |
| **Rejected** | Declined — does not affect your hours |
| **Expired** | The request date passed without a decision |
| **Archived** | Manually moved to archive by you |

---

### Managing Requests

- **Archive** — move a resolved request out of the active list. Useful for keeping the list tidy.
- **Bulk archive** — check multiple requests and archive them all at once.
- **Delete** — permanently removes an archived request. This cannot be undone.

---

### Viewing Request Decisions

When a supervisor approves or rejects a request, a notification appears in **Recent Activity** on your Dashboard. Click **View** on that activity item to jump to the specific request.

## Documents

The Documents page is your personal file storage within IMS.

### Uploading Files

1. Navigate to **Documents**.
2. Click **Upload** and select a file from your device.
   - Or click **Add Link** to attach a web URL instead of a file.
3. Choose a destination folder (defaults to **My Documents** root).
4. Confirm the upload.

---

### Managing Folders

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Create folder | Click **New Folder**, enter a name, confirm |
| Navigate into folder | Click the folder name |
| Rename folder | Use the rename action next to the folder |
| Delete folder | Use the delete action next to the folder |

---

### Sharing Documents

1. Click the **Share** icon on any file.
2. Choose a visibility mode:

| Mode | Who can see the file |
|------|---------------------|
| **Private** | Only you |
| **Everyone** | All users in the system |
| **Specific users** | Search and select individual users |
| **Everyone except** | All users except selected individuals |

3. Copy the share link from the dialog to send directly to someone.

---

### Searching

Use the **Search** bar at the top of the Documents page to filter files by name. The search covers all folders.

## Settings

Access **Settings** from the sidebar to manage your account and preferences.

### Profile

- Update your **Full Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Department**.
- Upload a **Profile Photo** by clicking the camera icon.
  - Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF
  - Maximum size: 5 MB
- Click **Save Changes** to apply.

### Security

- Enter your **Current Password** and a **New Password** to change your login credentials.
- The new password must be at least 8 characters.

### Notifications

Toggle which events send you a notification:

| Notification | Triggers when |
|-------------|--------------|
| Status Updates | A supervisor approves or rejects your request |
| Document Uploads | A document is shared with you |
| Time Log | Reminders related to your attendance |

### Supervisors

Displays the list of supervisors currently assigned to oversee your OJT. If this list is empty, ask your supervisor to assign you from the Intern Management page.
